Policy Statement

At Lumin Glass LLC, we strive to ensure every client is satisfied with our products and services. Our projects involve the fabrication and installation of custom glass systems made specifically for each client’s project specifications.

Because all materials are custom-ordered and fabricated to fit exact measurements, refunds are not typically available once production has begun.

If a project is cancelled before material orders or fabrication start, a partial refund may be issued minus any design, administrative, or sourcing costs already incurred.

Any refund or adjustment request must be submitted in writing to info@luminglass.com within 7 days of cancellation.

In the rare event that a service issue arises, we will work directly with the client to correct or replace any defective work to ensure satisfaction and compliance with the agreed project scope.

Client Acknowledgement

Clients confirm their understanding and acceptance of this policy by approving their invoice and project proposal via email prior to project start.
